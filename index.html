Independent research

Lunar Titanium × Magnetism

Python

I wanted to know if titanium-rich patches on the Moon line up with its leftover magnetism. I used spatially-blocked ML so the test couldn't fool itself — and it came back inconclusive: not enough signal to say either way. I wrote it up honestly instead of forcing a result.
